A reference photo is a good start, but a useful consultation is a conversation. These five details help your stylist, barber, or nail artist translate inspiration into a result that works for you.

## 1. Bring more than one reference

Save one photo for the overall shape, one for the detail you love, and—if possible—one example of what you do not want. Mention what you like about each image rather than asking for an exact copy.

## 2. Describe your routine

Share how often you wash, style, or visit the salon, and what tools or products you actually use. A look that needs an hour each morning is not low-maintenance just because it is short.

## 3. Be clear about comfort

Mention tender areas, scalp sensitivity, preferred nail length, or any finish you find uncomfortable. Agree on the shape, tension, and maintenance plan before the service begins.

## 4. Set a realistic upkeep plan

Ask how the style changes as it grows out, how to care for it at home, and when to book a refresh. If the plan does not suit your calendar, ask for a simpler variation.

## 5. Agree on the details before you start

Confirm length, colour, parting, nail shape, finish, and any add-ons. If there is a cost difference between options, ask about it before work begins.

The best result comes from matching the inspiration to the person in the chair. Bring your questions, make space for your artist’s expertise, and leave with a clear aftercare plan.
