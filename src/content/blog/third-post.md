Statement nails do not have to mean a busy design on every finger. A glossy finish, a soft colour shift, or one careful accent can make a set feel considered from across the room and rewarding up close.

## Glazed chrome

A sheer pink, caramel, or milky base with a fine reflective powder catches the light without overwhelming the colour underneath. Ask your nail artist which chrome finish suits the base shade you have chosen.

## Aura fades

Blend a softly diffused colour into the centre of a neutral nail for a gentle halo effect. Muted rose, plum, cocoa, or a warm orange can all work; choose one accent shade and keep the rest of the palette calm.

## Micro-French tips

A fine, narrow tip gives a familiar French manicure a lighter profile. Try espresso, berry, or soft gold rather than defaulting to bright white.

## One detailed accent

Tiny metallic lines, a small raised detail, or a single gem can add dimension. One or two accent nails keep the whole set wearable and leave the design room to breathe.

## Choose the shape for your week

Short oval and squoval shapes are practical for typing and everyday tasks. Almond or tapered shapes create a longer line, while extra-long extensions need more care around bags, zips, and hard surfaces. Bring a photo and ask your nail artist to adapt the length and design to your natural nail bed.

### Keep the set looking fresh

Use cuticle oil, wear gloves for cleaning, and avoid using extensions as tools. Never peel or pry off an enhancement; ask a qualified nail professional about safe removal and give your natural nails a break if they feel sore or damaged.
