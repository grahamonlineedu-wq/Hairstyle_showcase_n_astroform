“Hair set” can mean a ready-to-wear wig, bundles for a sew-in, clip-in extensions, or crochet hair. Before choosing a texture or length, decide how you want to wear it and how much daily styling you want to do.

## Lace-front wig

A lace-front unit gives you a defined front hairline and can be worn in several parting styles, depending on its construction. Check the cap size, lace shade, density, and whether the unit is designed for glue, tape, or a glueless fit. A professional fitting can help with comfort and a natural-looking finish.

## Closure or frontal bundles

Bundles are installed with a closure or frontal to create a sew-in. A closure usually means a smaller lace area and a simpler routine; a frontal offers more parting flexibility but needs careful handling around the lace. Confirm the number of bundles and finished length with your stylist before buying.

## Clip-in extensions

Clip-ins add volume or length for a day or an occasion and can be removed at home. Ask for a texture and colour match in natural light. If your hair is fine or fragile, use them sparingly and avoid attaching clips to the same small section every time.

## Curly and coily units

Texture-matched curly or coily units can blend beautifully with natural hair. Look at the curl pattern when dry, not only when freshly styled, and ask how much the hair shrinks after washing. A few minutes of detangling with conditioner can make a big difference to upkeep.

## Synthetic, blended, or human hair?

Synthetic units are often ready-styled and budget-friendly, but many cannot tolerate heat. Blended fibres vary, so check the maker’s heat guidance. Human-hair units allow more restyling but need washing, conditioning, and protection from excess heat—much like your own hair.

### A useful buying checklist

- Confirm cap size, attachment method, length, density, and colour.
- Ask whether the fibre can be heat-styled and how to wash it.
- Check the return policy before altering lace or removing tags.
- Bring the unit to your stylist before installation if you are unsure.

Choose the set that fits the way you actually get dressed. A beautiful unit is the one you feel confident wearing and can maintain comfortably.
